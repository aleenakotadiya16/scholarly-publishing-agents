# AI Disclosure Template — Elsevier

## Section Title

**Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process**

## Placement

Place this section **before the References**, as a separate section with the heading above.

## Template

---

### Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process

During the preparation of this work the author(s) used **[AI TOOL NAME AND VERSION]** in order to **[SPECIFIC PURPOSE — e.g., "improve language clarity and readability," "assist with literature identification," "generate standardized statistical methods descriptions"]**.

**[If multiple tools, add a sentence for each]:**
The author(s) also used **[ADDITIONAL TOOL AND VERSION]** for **[SPECIFIC PURPOSE]**.

After using this tool/service, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the publication.

---

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Language editing only

> During the preparation of this work the author(s) used Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026) in order to improve language clarity and grammar in the Introduction and Discussion sections. After using this tool, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the publication.

### Example 2: Literature search and writing

> During the preparation of this work the author(s) used ChatGPT-4o (OpenAI, December 2025) in order to assist with literature identification and language editing. All identified references were independently verified by the authors against PubMed and CrossRef databases. After using this tool, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the publication.

### Example 3: Comprehensive use

> During the preparation of this work the author(s) used Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026) in order to: (1) assist with literature search and reference identification; (2) improve language clarity in the Introduction, Methods, and Discussion sections; (3) check formatting compliance with journal requirements. All references identified with AI assistance were independently verified against CrossRef and PubMed. The AI tool was not used for data analysis, results generation, or interpretation of findings. After using this tool, the author(s) reviewed and edited the content as needed and take(s) full responsibility for the content of the publication.

## Notes

- Elsevier uses a specific section heading — do not deviate from "Declaration of Generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the writing process"
- This section should appear after Author Contributions and before References
- The closing sentence "After using this tool/service..." is Elsevier's standard language
- Some Elsevier journals (e.g., The Lancet, Cell) may have additional requirements — always check the specific journal's author guidelines
- If no AI tools were used, this section is not required
