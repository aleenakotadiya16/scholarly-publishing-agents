# AI Disclosure Template — Wiley

## Placement

Wiley typically requires AI disclosure in the **Acknowledgements section** or a **dedicated AI disclosure section** (check specific journal guidelines).

## Template

---

### Acknowledgements

The authors disclose the use of **[AI TOOL NAME, PROVIDER, VERSION]** during the preparation of this manuscript. The tool was used for **[SPECIFIC PURPOSE(S)]**. All content produced with AI assistance was reviewed, verified, and edited by all authors to ensure accuracy and integrity. The authors take full responsibility for all content in this publication. **[IF APPLICABLE: No AI tools were used in the peer review of this manuscript.]**

---

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Language editing

> The authors disclose the use of Grammarly (Grammarly Inc., 2025) and Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026) during the preparation of this manuscript. Grammarly was used for grammar checking, and Claude was used for improving language clarity in the Discussion section. All suggested changes were reviewed and edited by all authors, who take full responsibility for all content in this publication.

### Example 2: Comprehensive use

> The authors disclose the use of Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026) during the preparation of this manuscript. The tool was used for: (1) literature search assistance, with all identified references independently verified against CrossRef; (2) language editing across all manuscript sections; (3) formatting compliance verification against journal requirements. The tool was not used for data analysis, statistical computation, or generation of results. All AI-assisted content was reviewed, verified, and substantially edited by all authors to ensure accuracy and integrity. The authors take full responsibility for all content in this publication.
