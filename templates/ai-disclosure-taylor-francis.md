# AI Disclosure Template — Taylor & Francis

## Placement

Taylor & Francis requires an **AI disclosure statement** submitted as part of the manuscript submission process. Some journals also require it within the manuscript (typically in a dedicated section or Acknowledgements). Check the specific journal's submission guidelines.

## Template

---

### AI Statement (for submission form)

The author(s) confirm(s) the following AI-assisted technologies were used in the preparation of this manuscript:

**AI Tool(s) Used**: [AI TOOL NAME, PROVIDER, VERSION]

**Purpose(s)**: [SELECT ALL THAT APPLY]
- [ ] Language editing and proofreading
- [ ] Literature search and reference identification
- [ ] Data analysis assistance
- [ ] Code generation
- [ ] Figure/visualization creation
- [ ] Translation
- [ ] Other: [SPECIFY]

**Description of Use**: [PROVIDE SPECIFIC DETAILS OF HOW AI WAS USED]

**Human Oversight**: The author(s) confirm(s) that all content generated or modified with AI assistance was reviewed, verified, and edited by the author(s), who take(s) full responsibility for the content and integrity of this work.

### In Manuscript (Acknowledgements or dedicated section)

The authors used **[AI TOOL NAME, PROVIDER, VERSION]** to assist with **[SPECIFIC PURPOSE]** during the preparation of this manuscript. All AI-generated or AI-edited content was reviewed, verified, and approved by all authors. The authors take full responsibility for the content and accuracy of this work.

---

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Submission form — language editing

> **AI Tool(s) Used**: Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026)
> **Purpose(s)**: Language editing and proofreading
> **Description of Use**: Claude was used to improve the clarity and grammar of the Introduction and Discussion sections. All suggestions were reviewed and selectively adopted by the authors.
> **Human Oversight**: All authors reviewed the final manuscript and take full responsibility for its content.

### Example 2: In-manuscript disclosure

> The authors used Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026) to assist with language editing and formatting compliance during the preparation of this manuscript. All AI-generated suggestions were reviewed, evaluated, and edited by all authors, who take full responsibility for the content and accuracy of this work.

## Notes

- Taylor & Francis may provide a specific submission form for AI disclosure
- AI technologies cannot be listed as authors
- Transparent disclosure is required regardless of the extent of AI use
- Check individual journal guidelines for additional requirements
- T&F policies are regularly updated — verify current requirements before submission
