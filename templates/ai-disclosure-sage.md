# AI Disclosure Template — SAGE

## Placement

SAGE typically requires AI disclosure in the **Acknowledgements** or **Methods section** (check specific journal guidelines).

## Template

---

### Acknowledgements

The author(s) declare(s) the use of **[AI TOOL NAME, PROVIDER, VERSION]** during the preparation of this manuscript. The AI tool was used for **[SPECIFIC PURPOSE(S)]**. All content generated with AI assistance was reviewed, verified for accuracy, and edited by the author(s). The author(s) take(s) full responsibility for the content of this publication, including any portions that were generated with AI assistance.

### Methods (if AI used in analysis)

**[AI TOOL NAME, PROVIDER, VERSION]** was used to **[DESCRIBE ANALYTICAL USE — e.g., "generate R code for the statistical analysis," "assist with thematic coding of qualitative data"]**. All AI-generated outputs were independently verified by **[AUTHOR NAME(S)]**. The final analyses and interpretations are the sole responsibility of the authors.

---

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Writing assistance (Acknowledgements)

> The authors declare the use of Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026) during the preparation of this manuscript. The AI tool was used for language editing and improving readability of the manuscript text. All content was reviewed, verified for accuracy, and edited by all authors. The authors take full responsibility for the content of this publication.

### Example 2: Analysis and writing (Methods + Acknowledgements)

> **In Methods**: R code for the mixed-effects regression analysis was generated with assistance from Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026). All code was reviewed, tested, and validated by [Author Name] prior to execution. The final analysis and interpretation are the sole responsibility of the authors.
>
> **In Acknowledgements**: The authors also used Claude 3.5 Sonnet for language editing of the Introduction and Discussion sections. All AI-assisted text was reviewed, verified, and edited by all authors.

---

## Notes

- SAGE does not permit AI to be listed as an author
- Full disclosure of AI assistance is required
- Authors must verify all AI-generated content
- SAGE provides guidance on acceptable vs. unacceptable uses of AI
- Check individual SAGE journal guidelines for specific requirements or formats
- SAGE journals may use a structured submission form for AI declarations
