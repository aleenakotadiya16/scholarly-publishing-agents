# AI Disclosure Template — Springer Nature

## Placement

Springer Nature typically requires AI disclosure in the **Methods section** or **Acknowledgements** (check the specific journal's guidelines).

## Template

---

### In Methods Section (when AI was used for analysis or methodology)

The authors used **[AI TOOL NAME, PROVIDER, VERSION]** during the preparation of this manuscript for **[SPECIFIC PURPOSE]**. Specifically, **[DESCRIBE HOW THE TOOL WAS USED AND IN WHICH SECTIONS]**. All AI-generated content was critically reviewed and edited by all authors, who take full responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the work. **[IF APPLICABLE: All references identified with AI assistance were independently verified against CrossRef and PubMed databases.]**

### In Acknowledgements (when AI was used for writing/editing only)

The authors acknowledge the use of **[AI TOOL NAME, PROVIDER, VERSION]** for **[SPECIFIC PURPOSE — e.g., "language editing and improving manuscript readability"]**. All content was reviewed, edited, and approved by all authors, who take full responsibility for the published work.

---

## Usage Examples

### Example 1: Language editing (Acknowledgements)

> The authors acknowledge the use of Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026) for language editing and improving manuscript clarity. All suggested edits were reviewed, evaluated, and modified as needed by all authors, who take full responsibility for the published work.

### Example 2: Analysis and writing (Methods)

> The authors used Claude 3.5 Sonnet (Anthropic, January 2026) during the preparation of this manuscript for the following purposes: (1) assistance with constructing systematic literature search strategies; (2) language editing of the Introduction and Discussion sections; (3) generation of standardized descriptions of statistical methods. All AI-assisted content was critically reviewed and substantially edited by all authors. All references were independently verified against CrossRef and PubMed. The authors take full responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the work.

---

## Notes

- Springer Nature requires that AI tools are NOT listed as authors or co-authors
- Large language models must not be used to process confidential peer review material
- AI-generated images are not permitted for non-research illustration purposes
- Check specific journal (e.g., Nature, Nature Medicine) for additional requirements
- Include the AI tool version and the date/period of use where possible
