# Dissemination — Plain-Language Summaries and Outreach

## Purpose

Maximize the impact and reach of published research by creating accessible summaries and dissemination materials for different audiences.

---

## Dissemination Materials

### 1. Plain-Language Summary

Convert the research findings into language accessible to the general public.

**Target**: Reading level of grade 8-10 (Flesch-Kincaid Grade 8-10)

**Structure**:
```markdown
## [Title in plain language]

### What was the question?
[1-2 sentences: What problem were you trying to solve?]

### What did we do?
[2-3 sentences: How did you study this? What data did you use?]

### What did we find?
[2-3 sentences: What are the main findings in non-technical language?]

### What does this mean?
[2-3 sentences: Why does this matter? Who does it affect? What should happen next?]
```

**Example** (for a health economics paper):
```markdown
## Is video doctor visits a good use of healthcare dollars in rural areas?

### What was the question?
We wanted to know whether offering doctor appointments by video call (telehealth)
saves money compared to traditional in-person visits for people living in rural areas.

### What did we do?
We used a computer model to compare the costs and health benefits of telehealth
versus in-person care for 10,000 simulated patients over 5 years. We included
costs of technology, travel savings, and health outcomes.

### What did we find?
Telehealth saved an average of $1,200 per patient per year, mainly through
reduced travel costs and fewer missed work days. Patients using telehealth had
similar health outcomes to those seeing doctors in person.

### What does this mean?
Video appointments could be a cost-effective way to improve healthcare access
for people in rural areas. This could help guide government decisions about
funding telehealth services.
```

### 2. Social Media Posts

**Twitter/X (280 characters)**:
```
🔬 New study: Telehealth saves ~$1,200/patient/year in rural areas with similar health outcomes. Cost-effective at standard thresholds.

📄 [JOURNAL] [DOI link]
#HealthEconomics #Telehealth #RuralHealth
```

**LinkedIn (longer form)**:
```
Our new paper in [Journal] examines the cost-effectiveness of telehealth
in rural settings.

Key findings:
• Telehealth saved ~$1,200/patient/year
• Health outcomes comparable to in-person care
• Main savings from reduced travel and fewer missed work days
• Cost-effective at standard willingness-to-pay thresholds

This has important implications for healthcare policy in rural and
remote areas.

Full paper: [DOI link]

#HealthEconomics #Telehealth #RuralHealth #CostEffectiveness
```

### 3. Policy Brief (if applicable)

For research with policy implications:

```markdown
## Policy Brief: Telehealth Cost-Effectiveness in Rural Australia

### Key Message
Telehealth is a cost-effective alternative to in-person care for routine
consultations in rural areas, saving approximately $1,200 per patient annually.

### Background
[2-3 paragraphs on the policy context]

### Evidence
[Summary of key findings with key numbers]

### Policy Implications
[Specific, actionable recommendations for policymakers]

### Recommendations
1. [Specific recommendation]
2. [Specific recommendation]
3. [Specific recommendation]

### Source
[Full citation with DOI]
```

### 4. Institutional Repository Deposit

Following the publisher's self-archiving policy:
- **Green OA**: Deposit the accepted manuscript (post-peer-review, pre-typesetting) in the institutional repository
- **Check embargo**: Some publishers require a 6-12 month embargo before self-archiving
- **Include metadata**: Full citation, DOI link to published version, license information

### 5. Research Data Repository

If applicable:
- Deposit research data in an appropriate repository (Figshare, Zenodo, Dryad, OSF)
- Link the data to the published paper via DOI
- Include a data dictionary and README
- Ensure the data availability statement in the paper points to the correct repository

## Audience-Specific Considerations

| Audience | Format | Language Level | Key Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| **General public** | Plain-language summary | Grade 8-10 | What and why it matters |
| **Policymakers** | Policy brief | Professional, non-technical | Implications and recommendations |
| **Clinicians** | Clinical summary | Professional, some technical | Clinical implications, practice changes |
| **Media** | Press release | Journalistic | Newsworthy angle, quotes |
| **Other researchers** | Conference abstracts, seminar slides | Technical | Methods, results, future directions |
| **Patients/consumers** | Infographic, FAQ | Simple | What it means for them |
